More practical questions ↗
Do I need to know what kind of help I need?

No. You can begin with what feels difficult or what you hope will feel different. The initial conversation can help clarify the options.

What if I do not feel a connection with a practitioner?

Fit is worth discussing. Share your concerns and ask about other options or a different approach. You can ask questions before deciding how to continue.

Can I ask about the approach before booking?

Yes. Ask about areas of focus, how sessions work, and the practical details. You should have the information you need to make a considered choice.

Do I have to share everything straight away?

You can begin with a brief explanation and ask how information is handled. Take time to discuss your comfort, boundaries, and questions with the practitioner.
