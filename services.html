Before you begin

A few things
you might wonder.

You do not have to figure everything out before getting in touch.

Ask your own question ↗
How do I know which service to choose?

Start with what is happening or what you would like support with. You can ask the care team about the different options before deciding on a starting point.

What should I ask before booking?

Ask about fees, session length, format, availability, cancellation terms, and the practitioner's qualifications and approach. Confirm these details directly before making a commitment.

Can I discuss a different practitioner?

Yes. You can raise questions about fit and ask what other options are available. Availability and the best next step can be discussed with the care team.

What happens in the first conversation?

It is a chance to talk about what brought you here, ask how support works, and discuss what you are looking for. You do not need to arrive with a complete account of everything.

How will my information be handled?

Ask the practitioner to explain confidentiality, its limits, record keeping, and how information is shared before you begin. Keep initial website enquiries brief and avoid including sensitive details.
